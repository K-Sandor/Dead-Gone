Base supplies are kept in a Storage building. Walk to it in your camp to store or take items.

Game data and notes
  • One loot run = entering and leaving one building. Travelling costs hunger and thirst, more the further you go; each building costs a little.
  • Zombies notice you by sight or by noise (moving, searching, fighting). Gunshots alert every zombie in the building.
  • A day passes when you sleep at base; each night costs some hunger and thirst. A building restocks 3 sleeps after you first go in; until then killed zombies stay dead and searched containers stay empty.
  • Stats grow by use: Stealth from melee hits on zombies that haven't noticed you, Shooting from shots that hit, Agility from zombie blows you dodge by stepping out of reach during the wind-up. Agility makes you move faster.
  • Zombie blows always land if they reach you. Step out of reach during the wind-up (the red circle), or hold Block (F on a keyboard) with a melee weapon in hand: blows from the front are stopped, you move at half speed and cannot attack. Each blow you stop costs Stamina (the blue bar). If a blow empties the bar you are stunned for a moment and cannot move, attack or block. Stamina comes back by itself, at half speed while you hold Block. A melee swing also plants your feet for a moment.
  • Each building has its own speciality: food at the grocery store and gas station, medicine at the pharmacy, clothing and backpacks at the clothing store, Bricks and tools at the hardware store, guns and ammo at the police station (which also holds more zombies). Wear a backpack from the Equipment page for extra slots. Protective gear: armor takes damage off every zombie hit, head gear prevents some wounds, gloves prevent some bleeding.
  • Head out opens the world map (flat, not 2.5D). Polis is the city; the villages and farms are smaller, with fewer zombies and less loot. A journey costs hunger and thirst by distance (going back to your base costs half) and is a short loading screen.
  • Trees in the base (and the forest in the south-west corner) block walking and building. Find a Splitting axe (crates, cabinets) or make an Improvised axe at a Tool shop, equip it, then walk to a tree and tap it to chop it down for Wood. Wood builds wooden walls. Scrap metal drops from crates and boxes and is used for the workshop (3×3, Wood + Scrap metal).
  • Base: you arrive in your camp and walk around it with the left side of the screen (or WASD); drag the right side to look around. Walk up to the shelter to upgrade it or sleep, or to a storage to use it, then tap it or press the button that appears (E on a keyboard). Pick Wall or Storage to build on the 50×50 grid. Building is instant and costs Bricks from your bag and base storage. Removing gives no refund.
  • Survivors: build a radio station. Each night it may pick up a distress call; the world map and the place's map show where (SOS). Go into that building, reach the survivor, press Rescue and leave alive. Survivors live in the shelter (two beds per level), eat 1 food and 1 drink from base storage each night, and can be given a job from the shelter or the radio: Guard, Farmer, Hunter, Scavenger. Each survivor has their own attributes and skills (0 to 200): Guard uses Shooting and Melee, Farmer Strength and Stamina, Hunter Shooting and Stealth, Scavenger Agility and Lockpicking. Higher stats bring more, up to double, and the stats of a job grow a little every day it is worked. A hunter needs a gun or a bow: hand one over in the Survivors list. A gun uses 1 Ammo from base storage per Raw meat; a bow needs Arrows in storage and loses one now and then.
  • Every gun takes its own ammo: Glock and Beretta 9mm, Colt 1911 .45 ACP, Revolver .357 Magnum, Hunting rifle .308, M4A1 5.56, AK47 7.62. Only the right type reloads a gun, and a hunter or guard with a gun needs that type in base storage. The Ammo depot makes every type.
  • The bow is quiet: a shot does not wake the building, and a zombie that hasn't noticed you takes double damage. It holds one Arrow, and about half the Arrows you shoot are picked up again when you leave. Bows and Arrows are made at the Tool shop or found on tool racks and in gun lockers.
  • Jobs: Guard, Farmer, Hunter, Scavenger, Woodcutter (Wood every day), Medic (heals you and hurt survivors at night and treats bleeding and wounds from storage) and Radio operator (more distress calls). Guards fight raids beside you in the yard and can be hurt or killed: hand each one a weapon in the Survivors list (a gun or bow shoots its own ammo from base storage). Hurt survivors heal a little every night, more on Rest or with a Medic; treat bleeding and wounds from the Survivors list.
  • Fresh food goes bad: Raw meat after 3 sleeps, Vegetables after 5. Cooked meals and tins keep, so cook what you cannot eat in time.
  • Defences: some raid zombies go for your buildings and batter them. A building has 100 HP and loses a little with every blow; the damage stays after the raid, and at 0 the building is wrecked and stops working. Walls they break down become rubble. Everything is repaired with Bricks (tap it). Walls have HP too (wooden 150, wall 300; tap one to raise it: reinforced 450, fortified 600, armored 800). A zombie that is after a building behind a wall batters the wall on its way to that building, not you. Traps kill one raid zombie each and must be set again; a watchtower near the shelter is a post for gun and bow guards (one per level, and 100 more HP per level), safe until zombies wreck it; tap a wall to reinforce it. A well gives Water every day.
  • Death is final: your save is wiped and a new game starts on day 1. The death screen shows how long you lasted, zombies killed, buildings cleared and more, next to your best game.
  • Raids grow by 4 zombies each time. From day 20 a quarter of raid zombies are brutes (dark red): 120 HP instead of 50, and every hit that lands does 10 more damage.
  • Salvage at the workshop: spare melee weapons give Scrap metal. With a Tool shop also spare guns (3 Scrap metal, two-handed 5), and 20 rounds of ammo give 1 Scrap metal. Cloth is kept for crafting and for bleeding. Survivors also come on their own: with a free bed and 5 nights of food and drink in storage for everyone, now and then someone walks in and asks to join.
  • Cooking: build a campfire (Wood and Bricks) and walk up to it. It turns Vegetables, Canned meat, Baked beans and Water into meals that restore more hunger, take one bag slot and, for the stews, give a little HP. Each meal burns 1 Wood. Herbal tea (1 Vegetables + 1 Water) restores 25 HP and 20 thirst: a way to heal that does not need medicine.
  • Farming: build a farm plot or greenhouse, then plant Seeds with Water and harvest Vegetables after a few sleeps. Seeds are found on the farms (produce crates and tool racks) and in the hardware store in Polis. A rain collector fills with Water while you sleep; tap it to take the Water.
